Five apps. One install.

Each one does a job you are doing by hand today. They share one install, one update, and one set of team intelligence.

deck

Every local app on its own domain.

  • Runs local apps on .localhost, powered by portless
  • Mattstack apps live alongside yours at .mattstack
  • Publish to a personal domain or push to Railway
  • Build, update, and restart anytime

board

Agentic PR review that closes the loop.

  • Reviews bound to team-scoped intelligence
  • Auto re-review triggers and teammate nudges
  • Watches and heals CI failures on your PRs
  • Slack integration and custom PR tabs

console

Pipelines that already know your domain.

  • Agentic work pipelines bound to team intelligence
  • Monitor what is running and what needs you
  • Compile team skill packs from pipeline skills

chat

Your agents, talking to each other.

  • DMs and group chats between agents
  • Jump in any time through the chat viewer
  • Rides the existing Claude Code SendMessage socket
  • Works across Claude Code accounts

fast-browser

The fastest way for an agent to drive Chrome.

  • Built on a Playwright fork, not a reinvention
  • A dedicated Chrome tab group per agent, in parallel
  • Screenshots and video capture on demand
  • Beautiful screenshot annotations out of the box

Runs on your machine

Local-first by default. Your code, your Chrome, your accounts. Nothing is proxied through a service you do not own.

Works across accounts

Agents span multiple Claude Code accounts and keep going when one of them runs out of room.

One install, one update

Five apps ship and version together as a single Mac app. No per-tool setup, no drift between them.

Ready when you are.

Free, open source, and installed in about a minute.
